Computer Vision Algorithms & Methodology
  • 1. Face Recognition & Embedding Engine:
    Extracts facial feature embeddings (128-dimensional vector) from detected face bounding boxes and computes Cosine Similarity against registered student embeddings in SQLite.
  • 2. Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) Eye Tracking:
    Uses 6 key landmarks around left and right eye contours from MediaPipe 468 face mesh. Calculates vertical-to-horizontal distance ratio to detect eyes open, blinking, and prolonged eye closure (drowsiness).
  • 3. 3D Head Pose Estimation (Perspective-n-Point):
    Maps 2D landmarks (nose tip, chin, eye corners, mouth corners) to 3D canonical facial model coordinates using OpenCV cv2.solvePnP. Decomposes rotation matrix to extract Euler angles (Pitch, Yaw, Roll).
Privacy & Ethical Considerations
Institutional Compliance Notice:
  • Face data and embeddings are collected exclusively for authorized classroom attendance.
  • Attention scores are visual estimates based on eye openness and head orientation, not psychological state measurements.
  • Raw webcam video streams are processed in memory and never saved to persistent storage.
  • System should be used with student consent and institutional policy approval.
System Requirements:
Python 3.x, Flask, OpenCV 4.x, MediaPipe 0.10.x, NumPy, SQLite3, Modern Web Browser with HTML5 Video support.
